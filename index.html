Highway Surfer – Account Deletion Guide

If you no longer wish to use Highway Surfer, you can permanently delete your account by following the steps below. Please note that this action is irreversible.

Step 1: Open the Highway Surfer game and navigate to the main menu screen.
Step 2: Tap the Settings button located at the bottom of the main screen. Then, tap the Account button on the left side of the screen.
Step 3: The Account popup will display your username (if you are signed in) along with the Delete Account button. Tap Delete Account to begin the deletion process.
Step 4: A confirmation popup will appear asking you to confirm the deletion of your account. Tap the Yes button to permanently delete your account and all associated data.
Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, all game progress and associated data will be permanently removed.
